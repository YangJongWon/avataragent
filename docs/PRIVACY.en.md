# Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026

YangJongWon ("we") runs the AvatarCast site at avatarcast.app (the "site"). We handle your personal information with care and follow the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and the laws that apply where you live, such as the EU and UK GDPR. This policy covers the site only. The AvatarCast Office app you install on your own PC keeps its data on that PC; we do not receive it.

## 1. What we collect and why

| When | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signing up and signing in | Email address, name, profile picture URL, sign-in method (email, Google or GitHub) and that account's identifier | Creating your account, verifying it's you, keeping you signed in |
| Editing your profile | Handle, bio, links | Showing your public profile |
| Using the community and forum | Theme, workflow and playbook files you upload and their descriptions, posts, comments, likes, reports | Running the service, computing rankings and badges, moderation and handling reports |
| Buying a plan or a theme | Payment status and amount, subscription period, the install ID of the app the license applies to, the payment provider's transaction reference | Processing payments, issuing and renewing licenses, refunds, paying creators |
| Getting creator payouts | Payout details such as a bank account (collected separately by email) | Paying out your earnings |
| While you use the site | Sign-in cookie, IP address (used only for rate limiting, not stored), country you connect from (used only to choose the display language, not stored) | Security, abuse prevention, choosing the display language |

Card numbers and other payment-method details go directly to our payment provider, Paddle; we never see or store them. We use no advertising or behavioral analytics tools.

## 2. Cookies and browser storage

- `site_session`: keeps you signed in (30 days). Signing out removes it.
- A short-lived signed cookie during Google/GitHub sign-in, to prevent forgery (a few minutes).
- `siteLanguageChoice` and `siteLanguageByCountry` in browser storage: remember your display language. They are never sent to the server.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account and profile: until you ask us to delete your account. We delete it promptly when you ask.
- Sign-in sessions: end after 30 days or when you sign out. Email sign-in links work for 15 minutes.
- Uploads, posts and comments: until you delete them or your account.
- Payment, subscription and refund records: for the period the law requires (for example, under Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, 5 years for payment and supply records and 3 years for complaint and dispute records). These are kept separately for that period even if you delete your account.
- Reports and moderation records: as long as needed to handle disputes and abuse (up to 3 years).

When the period ends, we delete the information so it cannot be restored.

## 5. What is public

Your profile (handle, name, picture, bio, links, join date, badges), uploads, posts and comments are visible to everyone. Your email address is never shown.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. If you are in the EU, the UK or a similar jurisdiction, you also have the right to data portability, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. You can edit your profile on the site yourself; for anything else, email us at the address below. Once we've confirmed it's you, we act promptly (within 10 days, or within one month where the GDPR applies).

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performance of a contract (accounts, payments, licenses), legal obligation (keeping payment records) and legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, handling reports).

## 7. How we protect it

- Sign-in tokens and sessions are stored only as hashes, and cookies are sent HttpOnly.
- The production site is served over HTTPS only.
- Admin features are limited to designated administrators, and admin actions are logged.
- Secrets are kept in environment variables, never in the code.

## 8. Children

The site is not meant for children under 14 (or under the age your country sets, such as in the EU). Please don't sign up if you are under that age. If we learn that we hold such information, we delete it.
